## Review standard

`PROMOTE` requires all four conditions: a real historical failure, a unique reliability dimension, deterministic grading, and no existing-case coverage. `KEEP` means the candidate remains valuable but needs stronger evidence or a more explicit grading rule before becoming official. `REJECT` is reserved for unsupported, redundant, or non-gradeable candidates.

## Decisions

### case_201_observation_failure_must_not_trigger_business_recovery

- decision: **PROMOTE**
- reason: A real repeated screenshot-channel failure led to new-tab, reload, and produce-resume recovery while title/URL reads showed the tab was alive. This is not redundant with ARB002: ARB002 separates observation failure from action outcome, while case_201 tests the narrower recovery-scope boundary and prevents observation failure from triggering business-touching recovery. The expected classification, liveness condition, and prohibited escalation are deterministic and directly supported by the failure record, timings, and hardened screenshot contract.

### case_202_crash_backfill_evidence_durability_downgrade

- decision: **PROMOTE**
- reason: A real crash left the original screenshot directory empty and later claims were reconstructed from transcripts with an explicit non-original/raw downgrade. This is distinct from ARB006's stale metadata precedence: it tests evidence durability, provenance downgrade, and the treatment of call-ID references. Grading is deterministic: preserve the raw-evidence gap, cite backfill as reconstructed/non-durable, reject reconstructed claims as original first-hand frames, and keep unresolved claims UNKNOWN.

### case_203_noop_kettei_single_retry_boundary

- decision: **KEEP**
- reason: The no-op submit followed by one retry was recorded twice, so duplicate-action risk is real and the dimension is not covered by existing cases. However, both retries were benign and the records do not establish an authoritative rule for when one retry is permissible. Promoting now would make the gold answer depend on an unratified retry policy. Keep until the retry boundary is explicitly anchored or a violating occurrence is recorded.

### case_204_registry_staleness_active_run_resolution

- decision: **KEEP**
- reason: Registry staleness is a unique startup-authority hazard, but the candidate records no actual runtime failure or incorrect resolution. It is a latent counterfactual. Keep for a future case after a real stale-resolution incident or an explicitly approved adversarial benchmark track, with deterministic source precedence and mismatch recording.

### case_205_operator_evidence_provenance_classification

- decision: **KEEP**
- reason: Human evidence trust is a valuable and distinct reliability dimension, and the expected provenance distinction is crisp. The historical record shows the operator evidence was correctly classified and excluded from autonomous replication counts; no misclassification failure occurred. Keep as a negative-control/adversarial candidate until the benchmark explicitly admits such cases or a real promotion error is recorded.

### case_206_cross_battle_speed_persistence_assumption

- decision: **KEEP**
- reason: The candidate covers stale default assumptions across battles and is distinct from existing in-battle phase and ordering cases. The persistence observation is explicitly hypothesis-grade and n=1, with no recorded agent failure. Keep until independent re-observation or a causal failure makes the grading rule stable; do not freeze the suspected persistence as settled truth.
